What Is the Enneagram?

The Enneagram is a personality framework built around nine core types — each defined not just by behavior, but by the deeper motivations, fears, and desires that drive it. Unlike tests that sort you by surface traits, the Enneagram tries to explain the why behind who you are.

The word comes from Greek: ennea (nine) + grammos (figure). Each type has a core fear and a core desire — and that fear quietly shapes everything about how a person lives, works, and relates to others.

The Enneagram also includes wings (the neighboring types that color your personality) and stress and growth directions that show how you behave at your worst and your best.

It's not about boxing you in. It's about understanding the patterns you already live by — so you can choose them more consciously.
